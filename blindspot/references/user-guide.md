# Blindspot user guide

Blindspot checks the files on your NAS for things that could hurt you, cost you money, or waste space, and gives you one ranked report.

## Who it is for

Households, freelancers and small offices whose NAS has filled up over the years with screenshots, scans, contracts and invoices nobody has looked at since.

## What it looks for

- Passwords, recovery phrases and codes captured in screenshots or photos
- Passport, ID card and bank documents sitting in open folders
- Contracts that are about to end or renew automatically
- Invoices that charge more than the contract says, or arrive twice
- Duplicate files taking up space

## How to use it

Say something like "Check my Documents folder for blind spots" or "audit my NAS". Blindspot confirms the folders with you, runs the checks, and shows a report ranked from Critical to Info. Serious findings are checked a second time before they are shown, and sensitive values are always masked.

You can add your own house rules, such as "ID scans live only in Vault", and the report tells you whether each rule still holds. Run it again later and it shows what is new, fixed, downgraded or still open.

## What it will never do

- It never deletes a file.
- It never moves, tags or saves anything until you approve that exact change.
- It never sends your files outside the NAS. All analysis uses the experts on your own device.

## Limits

Results depend on the accuracy of on-device text recognition and document extraction. Blindspot can advise you to change a password or a sharing setting, but cannot do it for you.
